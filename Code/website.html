About Eta Omega Chi

Eta Omega Chi is a professional business fraternity at Babson College dedicated to developing the next generation of entrepreneurs and business leaders. We bring together driven individuals who share a commitment to excellence, integrity, and brotherhood.

Founded on the principles of academic achievement, professional development, and lasting bonds, our chapter has built a community of ambitious students who challenge and support one another — inside the classroom and beyond.

01

Brotherhood

Lifelong bonds built on trust, respect, and shared experience.

02

Professionalism

Preparing members for careers through networking and mentorship.

03

Excellence

Holding ourselves to the highest academic and personal standards.

04

Service

Giving back to the Babson and greater Boston community.

"We don't just build careers — we build each other."
